TOKEN1909 Jingzhang AI Nexus

An AI urban-design concept submission organized around three core pillars — Railway Heritage Backbone, Industry-University-City Suture, and Open-Source AI Urban Lab. AI carriers embed into old railway space without replacing heritage character; university / tech-park / residential boundaries are sutured so scenarios seep into daily blocks; the entire district becomes an iterable public test ground. This page renders overview map, three-tier scope, key areas, land-use, mobility, blue-green public space, building renewal, AI scenarios, core metrics, task coverage, self-check status, sources and assumptions into a human-readable visual brief.

01

Railway Heritage Backbone

Use the Jingzhang heritage corridor as the public main spine; AI embeds without replacing heritage character

Problem solvedDesign assumption (pending baseline surveys): the heritage strip appears cut by ring roads, property interfaces, and underpasses; only a landscape corridor with no functional spine; AI industry and heritage interaction is limited.
ImplementationNo large-scale demolition-and-rebuild. Developer promenade / AR anchors / edge compute embed into sleepers and under-bridge grey space; new carriers stay below the tree-canopy line.
Expected resultFrom a memorial strip into "an AI main street usable every day"; within a 10-minute walk there is always an AI interaction node + a heritage interpretation point.
02

Industry-University-City Suture

Open the physical boundaries between universities, tech parks, and residential neighborhoods; AI scenarios seep into daily blocks

Problem solvedDesign assumptions (commonly reported, pending baseline surveys): university gates, park walls, and residential fences form multiple breakpoints; AI industry interacts little with daily blocks; tech-transfer cycles are commonly described in years.
ImplementationA 9 km triple-parallel slow-mobility suture belt along the main spine; elevated connector corridors bridging three breakpoints; AI+ blocks scattered into community ground floors, campus backstreets, and rail micro-centers.
Expected resultFaculty/students reach an achievement release hall in a 5-minute walk; residents meet AI civic services on the way home; one-person startups launch from community "landing pods".
03

Open-Source AI Urban Lab

Turn the entire district into an iterable public test ground; Agents ship first, then iterate

Problem solvedMost AI parks are only "smart" on presentation boards and renderings; once built, every scenario goes offline. Citizens only ever meet AI as marketing interactive screens.
ImplementationDefine publicly testable geo-fences; ship T1 Investment Sandbox / T2 Digital Twin / T3 Unmanned Commerce — three Agent MVPs; interfaces are open to the public; iteration records join the annual report.
Expected resultOn a 3-year review revisit, three Agents are still running with iteration records; the Open-Source Contributor Plaza GitHub IDs grow year over year.

Overall Concept · Naming · One Belt Three Cores Two Wings · LOGO

Naming logic of TOKEN1909 / JAN (Jingzhang AI Nexus), the Beidaokou cultural subtitle, the three-core two-wing structure, and the "Ren"-rail motif LOGO sketch.

concept + identity

LOGO Sketch · "Ren"-rail Motif

"Ren"-rail motif: From Zhan Tianyou's 1909 "Ren"-shaped engineering to the AI node network; the three nodes represent L1 spiritual start / L2 innovation figures / L3 open-source contribution; the connecting line is the 9 km main spine.

Naming System

  • TOKEN1909: TOKEN symbolizes the value credential of the compute era; 1909 anchors the Jingzhang self-reliance spiritual origin.
  • JAN: Jingzhang AI Nexus; emphasizes the belt-like convergent and radiating hub attribute.
  • Subtitle Beidaokou — Tracks to Tomorrow: Beidaokou (around Wudaokou) as the cultural origin; from a century-old rail to the AI future.
  • Prefix + functional naming tree: Unified recognition across all nodes — e.g., JAN-Origin Community, JAN-Zhongzhiyuan, JAN-Dazhongsi.

Color system (concept): Rail ochre + Zhongguancun blue-green + highlight neon .

Three Cores & Two Wings · Relationship Diagram

Three-core loop: R&D source → exit strategy → commercial monetization, forming a closed value circuit. Two wings: the west wing (Zhongguancun S&T service) inputs capital / IP / globalization; the east wing (Xiaoyue River scenario empowerment) focuses on embodied AI / AI consumption; a "west-input east-use" synergy.
